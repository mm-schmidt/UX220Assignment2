<b>Canadian Ski Patrol</b>

- <b>What:</b> I volunteered with the Canadian Ski Patrol.

- <b>When:</b> since 2025.

- <b>What was good:</b> I developed advanced first-aid skills, got to ski for free, and made lasting friendships.

- <b>Favourite memory:</b> getting to ski on the one powder day Ontario has ever gotten with no one else at the hill.

- <b>What I didn’t enjoy:</b> long, cold days, especially when it rained.

- <b>Grade:</b> A
